Timing matters: Georgia requires the rebuilt inspection after repairs are finished but before the truck is painted. Repaired panels can be in primer. AMP does a full safety and structural inspection, so go through each item below and mark it Pass, Fix or N/A.

Bring with you

    Getting the truck there

    • A salvage-titled vehicle can't be registered or tagged, so ask AMP when you book whether you should tow or trailer the truck to the appointment.
    • Arrive with a full check: every light working, the SRS light going out after start-up and no warning lamps on the dash.
    • Leave the bed and cab empty so the inspector can see the floor pans, cab mounts and bed mounts.

    After you pass

    1. Keep the inspection paperwork AMP gives you.
    2. Go to your county tag office with the original salvage title, a signed MV-1, a signed T-129, your photos and receipts, and your ID.
    3. Georgia issues a title branded “Rebuilt”. Then register and tag the truck as usual.
    4. Emissions: In the 13 metro Atlanta counties (Cobb included), gasoline vehicles 3–24 model years old need a Georgia Clean Air Force emissions test to register. That's separate from AMP's inspection. Check whether your county and model year apply.
    5. Paint the truck now that it has passed.
